      How it works

      • Photos taken seconds apart that look alike form a burst.
      • Within a burst, photos are grouped into moments (same pose). The best photo of each moment is kept.
      • "Best" means: eyes open, face sharp enough, fewest blown-out highlights, then a bigger smile and facing the camera.
      • With several people in the photos, the app follows the one who appears most (outlined). Press Focus on a person to choose someone else.
      • The rest are moved to the holding folder. Nothing is deleted until you press the green button, and then only to the Recycle Bin.
      • Close calls are shown first: there the photos were very similar and you may prefer another.
      • Copies of one picture keep a single photo: the full-size one, then the one with its companion files, then the one with the most photo details (date, camera, place, rating), then a name without "copy".

      Keys

      Left/Right move between photos, Up/Down between bursts, K keep, X set aside, Space compare with the pick, Esc close, ? this help.
